Meet the Founder

Falcom Ombati

Founder & CEO, Kisii Dreadlocks Parlor

My name is Falcom Ombati and I am the proud founder of Kisii Dreadlocks Parlor. With over 8 years of hands-on experience in the locs industry, my passion for this craft goes far beyond styling — it is about celebrating identity, culture, and the beauty of natural hair.

My goal with every client is simple: to provide exceptional service, ensure healthy loc growth, and leave you feeling confident and satisfied every single time you walk out of our parlor. I believe that great locs are built on trust, skill, and consistency — and that is what we deliver.

We are conveniently located at Elimu Umoja Center, 2nd Floor, Kisii — and you are warmly welcome to visit us any day of the week. Whether you are starting your loc journey or maintaining an existing one, we are here for you.
